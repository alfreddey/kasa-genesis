# ADR NNNN: Short title of the decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Issues: #N (the issues this belongs to)

## Context

What is the situation, and what forces make a decision necessary? Name the constraints (budget, users, licences, team size). Two to six sentences.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, in the active voice ("We use ...").

## Alternatives considered

| Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chosen option** | Why it fits | Its costs |
| Another option | | Why we did not pick it |

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now do (new services, settings, follow-up issues), and what would make us revisit this.

---

How to use this folder:

- Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` with the next free number. Keep it to one page.
- A decision that is made stays in the folder. To change it, add a new ADR and set the old one's status to "superseded by ...". Do not rewrite history.
- Add an ADR for any new decision of the same size: a new service, language or framework, a licensing or hosting choice, or a change to how audio is made.
